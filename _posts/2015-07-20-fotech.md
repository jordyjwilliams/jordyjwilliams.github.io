I completed a seven-week placement with [Fotech Solutions](https://www.fotech.com/), a company specialising in Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) technology.

This role gave me hands-on experience in testing, validation, and quality assurance for externally produced CAD models and hardware units. I also created software to automate MATLAB-based tests and laser calibration workflows, and was responsible for delivering a complete end-to-end software output including documentation, a test plan, and a roadmap for future work.

* Testing, validation and QA of externally produced CAD models.
* Tolerance testing of hardware units.
* Report generation.
* Software development with the following aims:
  * Automating MAT tests.
  * Automating laser calibration.
* Independent delivery of a complete software project, including test plans, documentation and future work recommendations.

**Key skills:** Python, GUI development, test automation, QA, optical systems, MATLAB, technical documentation.

The placement strengthened my understanding of DAS and optical systems and gave me practical experience in applied engineering within a specialist sensing environment.
