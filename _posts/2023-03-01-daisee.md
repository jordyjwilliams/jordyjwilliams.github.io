I worked as a `Senior Software Engineer` at [daisee](https://www.daisee.com/), contributing in a fast-paced startup environment where reliability, maintainability and stakeholder alignment were all critical. Much of the work centred on client onboarding, customer support tooling, and improving legacy systems without sacrificing delivery speed.

A key focus was reducing friction between customer support and engineering teams by redesigning workflows, improving self-serve tooling, and removing recurring manual work. This simplified internal operations and reduced the time spent in cross-functional coordination and support escalations.

Roles and responsibilities:
* Streamlined client onboarding and ingestion with a focus on scalability.
* Implemented new scorecard question patterns using LLMs.
* Built internal tooling to reduce manual work and improve self-service for non-engineering stakeholders.
* Added telemetry, observability and CI/CD to legacy projects to improve safety and maintainability.
* Reduced technical debt and planned a more scalable standard ingestion pipeline.
* Improved code quality, unit testing practices and team culture around engineering standards.

**Key skills:** Software design, event-driven systems, Python, AWS, infrastructure as code (Terraform).
