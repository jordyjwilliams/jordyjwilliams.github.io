I undertook two separate placements with AECOM Acoustics teams, giving me exposure to both UK and New Zealand practice in a consultancy environment.

The work ranged from routine environmental monitoring to more analytical projects involving survey design, statistical analysis, and the interpretation of subjective response data.

* St Albans, UK: 15/08/2016 - 09/09/2016 (4 weeks):
   * Focused on building acoustics and client-facing technical work.
   * Worked with a broad range of national and international clients.
   * Carried out unattended and attended noise surveys across day and night periods.
* Christchurch, NZ: 01/06/2017 - 14/08/2017 (12 weeks):
   * Gained experience in the differences between New Zealand and UK acoustic regulations and practice.
   * Designed and analysed a noise annoyance survey to assess road traffic noise impacts.
      * Processed large datasets and identified trends in subjective annoyance results.
      * Contributed to a study that was, at the time, one of the first of its kind in New Zealand.
   * Authored a Python utility module to streamline survey analysis.
   * Implemented a networked real-time logging device to improve field data collection for noise surveys.

* Software used: `CadnA Acoustics`, `Rhino`, `CATTAcoustics`, `Odeon`, `SketchUp`, `AutoCAD`, `MATLAB`, `Python`, `Excel`, `VBA`.

**Key skills:** acoustic consultancy, noise and vibration assessment, survey design, data analysis, acoustic modelling, technical reporting, client communication.
