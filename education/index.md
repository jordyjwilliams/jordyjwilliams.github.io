## University Projects {#university-projects}

### Pearable

A freely programmable hearable designed and built with a cross-disciplinary team of seven engineers. Implemented on a Raspberry Pi 3B, it paired a Python server with a companion Android app built in Xamarin and C#.

I led the A2DP Bluetooth audio transfer work and built the mobile application.

[View the project app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.JJgames.pearable&hl=en) · [Browse the project code](https://bitbucket.org/hearablegdp/pearable-gdp-code/src)

![Pearable wearable audio project](/assets/img/pearable.png)

### Acoustic emissions and osteoarthritis

My individual acoustical engineering project explored signal processing of acoustic emissions to identify osteoarthritis in the knee. The project received the IMechE Best Project Certificate and was presented at a student conference.
